How to Play

How a match is won

  • You start with one base (the big star) and a small purse of gold.
  • Win by destroying every enemy base, or by holding 75% of the map.
  • The black line is the front. It follows your units, so it moves as they do.

Gold, not workers

  • Bases pay +4 gold/s, captured cities +2 gold/s. Nobody mines anything.
  • Every unit in the field costs 1 gold/s to keep. Units garrisoned inside a city are fed for free — that is what makes cities worth taking twice over.
  • Spend gold to build: light 30, heavy 85.
  • If upkeep outruns income your gold goes negative and the army starves. Take cities or shrink the army.

Morale and encirclement

Troops under sustained fire lose morale: they slow down and hit softer. Pull them back behind a base or city and they recover.

Cut a squad off from its own territory and it loses supply. Surrounded units die in seconds — encirclement kills faster than shooting.

Controls

  • LMB select · drag to box-select · double-click for all of that type
  • RMB move, or attack the unit under the cursor
  • A attack-move · S stop · H hold · Z select whole army
  • Q/W build light/heavy at the selected base · E pause its production
  • Select a base, then RMB on the map to set its rally point
  • Tab cycle bases · F centre on selection · Ctrl+1..9 groups
  • Arrows / screen edge / middle-drag to pan · wheel to zoom · Space pause

On touch: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a unit or city to select, tap the ground to move. Pan/Select switches dragging to a selection box, Attack makes your next tap an attack-move, and a long press does the same without the button.
