How to Play

The whole game in four lines

  • Cities build units by themselves. You choose what they build.
  • Each city supports 5 units. Go over the cap and your furthest troops starve.
  • Stand next to an undefended city to capture it. More cities, more army.
  • Kill everything the enemy owns, or take every city, and the battle is yours.

The ground

  • Plains — open. Everything works here, heavies best of all.
  • Forest — passable, but heavies crawl and barely hurt anything. Light infantry takes cover here.
  • Hills — same penalty as forest, slightly slow for everyone.
  • Water and mountains — impassable. Nothing crosses except by bridge.
  • Roads and bridges — faster marching, and the only way over water.

The black line is the front: everything inside it is fed by your cities.

Units

Light — cheap and quick, fine in any terrain, and it even takes cover in rough ground. Wins through numbers.

Heavy — slow, expensive, brutal in the open. In forests it crawls and barely scratches anything. Use it to break lines and to hold clear ground.

Controls

  • LMB select · drag to box-select · double-click for all of that type
  • RMB move, or attack the unit under the cursor
  • A attack-move · S stop · H hold · Z select whole army
  • Q/W build light/heavy in the selected city · E pause its production
  • Select a city, then RMB on the map to set its rally point
  • Tab cycle cities · F centre on selection · Ctrl+1..9 groups
  • Arrows / screen edge / middle-drag to pan · wheel to zoom · Space pause

On touch: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a unit or city to select, tap the ground to move. Pan/Select switches dragging to a selection box, Attack makes your next tap an attack-move, and a long press does the same without the button.
